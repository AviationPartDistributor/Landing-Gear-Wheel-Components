# Aircraft Landing Gear & Wheel Components: Aviation Landing Gear Parts for MRO and Maintenance

Aircraft landing gear systems support ground operations, takeoff, landing, braking, and aircraft movement. Landing gear assemblies can include wheels, tires, brakes, struts, actuators, bearings, axles, torque links, steering components, and related hardware.

For aircraft operators and MRO organizations, dependable landing gear components are important for scheduled inspections, repair programs, replacement requirements, and aircraft maintenance.

## Aircraft Wheels

Aircraft wheels support the aircraft during taxiing, takeoff, and landing. Wheel assemblies can include wheel halves, bearings, spacers, seals, and associated hardware.

Correct wheel identification is essential because wheel assemblies vary according to aircraft type, landing gear configuration, and brake system.

## Aircraft Tires

Aircraft tires are designed for demanding landing and ground-operation conditions. Tire selection depends on aircraft application, wheel configuration, size, rating, and applicable technical requirements.

Maintenance organizations may manage tire replacement as part of scheduled landing gear maintenance.
